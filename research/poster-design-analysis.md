## Poster 1: Darwin Free Meditation A4 Flyer

Source: FreeMeditation Australia Darwin A4 PDF.

1. Background color: dominant deep forest green, approx `#1F6547` to `#246B4D`; bottom contact strip muted magenta/purple approx `#A64782`; top hero image green lotus pond.
2. Shri Mataji photo: small circular portrait, upper-right over the lotus, approx 13-15% page width. White circular border. She wears white shawl/sari, smiling, red bindi visible. Hands not visible. Label curves around circle: "Shri Mataji - founder."
3. Text elements: large hierarchy. Small "Sahaja Yoga" above very large "Free Meditation"; white condensed sans-serif. Quote on hero image in white with drop shadow. Mid-section benefits line: "Stress relief - Inner peace - Holistic health benefit." Date/venue uses bold sans-serif, centered.
4. Logo/symbol: subtle-system/chakra diagram appears as one of four small instructional tiles in the lower-middle. Not a formal logo lockup. QR code lower-left.
5. Color scheme: forest green, lotus pink, white, soft gray, muted purple/magenta strip, small multicolor chakra accents.
6. Layout structure: stacked vertical flyer. Header block -> hero lotus image with quote and founder portrait -> curved green divider -> benefits + explanation -> 4 instructional icons -> date/venue -> contact strip with QR.
7. Government approval / certificate mention: none visible.
8. "FREE" messaging: extremely prominent in headline: "Free Meditation" is the largest text on the poster. Secondary "Always Free" appears near bottom.
9. Date/venue/contact format: weekday/time first, then date range, venue name, street address, join-anytime reassurance, QR code, phone number, website.
10. Overall mood: spiritual-natural, calm, public-friendly, traditional, slightly devotional because of lotus/founder portrait/subtle-system tiles.

What works:
- "Free Meditation" is instantly readable.
- The green field gives high contrast to white text.
- QR + phone + website are grouped cleanly.

Risk for school/principal use:
- Lotus, Kundalini text, subtle-system image, and founder portrait make it visibly spiritual rather than secular/wellness.

## Repeated Design Patterns Across Posters

Standard format patterns:

1. Founder portrait is almost always present.
   - Usually circular, white-bordered, small-to-medium.
   - Common placements: upper-right over lotus, upper-center under title, or top-right in event banners.
   - In commemorative posters, Shri Mataji becomes the large hero image.

2. Green + white + lotus pink is the most repeated public-class palette.
   - Deep forest/olive green backgrounds: approx `#1F6547`, `#246B4D`, `#275000`.
   - White condensed sans-serif type.
   - Pink lotus hero imagery: approx `#F05BA7` to `#F8A3C6`.
   - Purple/magenta footer strips: approx `#A64782`.

3. "Free Meditation" is normally large and direct.
   - In public flyers, "Free Meditation" or "Free meditation classes" is often among the largest text.
   - Supporting reassurance appears as "Always Free", "No booking required", "Everyone welcome."

4. Contact information is bottom-banded.
   - QR code bottom-left.
   - Phone and website bottom-center/right.
   - High-contrast footer strip, often magenta/red/blue.

5. Layouts are stacked and centered.
   - Header/title at top.
   - Spiritual/nature hero image or founder image.
   - Benefit line or explanatory paragraph.
   - Date/venue/contact at bottom.

6. Subtle-system diagrams are common.
   - Often used as a visual explanation or identity marker.
   - Typically placed lower-middle or side column.
   - These are useful for practitioner audiences but risky for secular institutions.

7. Event banners use symmetrical trust identity.
   - NSYS/Yuvashakti posters place logo top-left and Shri Mataji portrait top-right.
   - Event title centered, date centered, venue centered.
   - Pastel gradient backgrounds are common.

8. Fonts skew condensed and formal.
   - Public flyers use tall condensed sans-serif for readable headlines.
   - Commemorative/founder flyers use serif uppercase and drop shadows.
   - Event banners use bold serif/display for seminar names.

9. Government approval text is not a repeated pattern.
   - No visible posters contained government approval or certificate mentions.
   - Institutional legitimacy is instead signaled through trust names, organizer names, QR/phone/website, and sometimes formal event language.

## What Works Visually

- Large direct free offer: "Free Meditation" is clear, low-friction, and trust-building.
- Founder portrait with circular white border creates a recognisable brand cue.
- Deep green + white has strong readability and a calm wellness association.
- Bottom CTA strip with QR, phone, website is practical and repeated.
- Event/date formatting is easy to scan when centered and bold.
- NSYS posters show a cleaner modern path: logo + portrait + pastel gradients + centered event facts.

---

## What Does Not Work For School / Principal Outreach

- Lotus flowers, subtle-system charts, chakra imagery, Sanskrit/spiritual education content, and founder-centric devotional framing can trigger religious concerns in schools.
- Dense paragraphs reduce notice-board readability.
- Too many small diagrams/icons make posters look instructional rather than administrative.
- Founder photo as dominant hero shifts attention from student benefit to movement identity.
- "Kundalini", "chakras", and "Self Realisation" are standard Sahaja terms, but they are not appropriate for secular school wellness first contact.

---

## Recommended Adaptation For School Principal Posters

Keep:

- Direct "Free Meditation Program" messaging.
- Clear bottom contact strip with QR, phone, website.
- Strong date/venue/session format.
- Calm green/white or blue/white palette.
- Simple organizer attribution.

Modify:

- Replace lotus/subtle-system visuals with real school/classroom wellness imagery.
- Use children/teacher/counselor visuals instead of founder-first layout.
- Move any Shri Mataji reference to a small optional credibility/footer note only if needed.
- Replace "Kundalini", "chakras", "Self Realisation" with secular language: focus, calm, stress reduction, emotional regulation, student wellbeing.
- Use Helvetica/Roboto/Open Sans rather than decorative or spiritual-styled fonts.

Avoid:

- Om, lotus, chakra/subtle-system diagrams, Sanskrit, deity-like lighting, auras, spiritual anatomy diagrams.
- Long quotes from Shri Mataji as the main headline for school audiences.
- Claims that imply treatment or guaranteed medical outcomes.

## Standard Public Sahaja Poster Formula

Most repeated structure:

1. Top: `Sahaja Yoga` small label + `Free Meditation` large headline.
2. Hero: lotus/nature image, often with Shri Mataji circular portrait.
3. Middle: short quote or benefit line.
4. Explanation: one paragraph about meditation, peace, balance, Kundalini/subtle system.
5. Optional visuals: subtle-system diagram or practice instruction thumbnails.
6. Event details: day/time/date, venue, address.
7. Footer: "Everyone welcome", "Always Free", "No booking required", QR, phone, website.

For school outreach, use the same information architecture but replace the spiritual visual language with secular school-wellness imagery.
