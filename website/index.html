Neuroscience Research

Measurable Brain Changes in 6 Weeks

EEG studies at King's College London show Sahaja Yoga meditation activates the prefrontal cortex — the brain's focus and decision-making center — while reducing activity in the amygdala, the stress and fear center.

  • Increased theta wave activity — linked to deep focus
  • Reduced cortisol levels — measurable stress reduction
  • Improved alpha-theta synchrony — associated with better memory consolidation

Source: Manocha R, et al. King's College London, 2011. Published in Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine.
